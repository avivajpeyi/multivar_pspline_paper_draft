{\footnotesize
\textbf{Notation:}
$\I^{(i)}(f_k)=\d^{(i)}(f_k)\d^{(i)}(f_k)^{*}$ is the periodogram of block $i$;\;
$\Y(f_k)=\sum_{i=1}^{N_b}\I^{(i)}(f_k)=N_b\bar{\I}(f_k)$;\;
$L_b=n/N_b$ block length;\; $T_b=L_b\Delta_t$ block duration;\;
$N_l=L_b/2$ block frequencies;\; $N_h=|J_h|$ frequencies per coarse bin.%
